## **Frontend Developer (React.js / Next.js)**

### **About Sakneen**

Sakneen is building cutting-edge real estate software that leverages AI, automation, and data-driven insights to transform how properties are marketed, priced, and managed. We’re looking for a passionate and skilled **Frontend Developer** to join our team and help deliver high-quality, reusable, and performant user interfaces.

---

### **Role Overview**

You will be responsible for developing, optimizing, and maintaining our frontend applications using **React.js**, **Next.js**, and **TypeScript**. You’ll collaborate closely with product managers, designers, and backend engineers to build scalable, maintainable, and visually appealing user experiences.

We value **clean code**, **component reusability**, and **performance optimization**.

---

### **Key Responsibilities**

* Develop new features and maintain existing ones across Sakneen’s web applications.
* Build reusable, testable, and efficient UI components with **React.js** and **Next.js (TSX)**.
* Integrate frontend applications with APIs using **RTK Query** (Redux Toolkit Query).
* Ensure responsiveness, cross-browser compatibility, and accessibility compliance.
* Optimize performance for speed and scalability.
* Work closely with the design team to implement high-fidelity UI/UX designs.
* Participate in code reviews, testing, and deployment processes.
* Write clear, maintainable, and well-documented code.

---

### **Requirements**

* **1+ years** of professional experience in frontend development.
* Strong proficiency in **React.js** and **Next.js** with **TypeScript (TSX)**.
* Solid experience with **RTK Query** or Redux Toolkit for state management and API integration.
* Strong understanding of **component-based architecture** and **UI reusability principles**.
* Experience with **Tailwind CSS** or similar modern CSS frameworks.
* Good understanding of **RESTful APIs** and asynchronous data handling.
* Familiarity with **code versioning tools** (Git, GitHub).
* Understanding of **performance optimization** and **SEO best practices** for Next.js.
* Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.

---

### **Nice to Have**

* Experience with **GraphQL** and Apollo Client.
* Familiarity with **Jest**, **React Testing Library**, or Cypress for frontend testing.
* Experience in building **design systems** and reusable UI libraries.
* Familiarity with **CI/CD pipelines** and deployment workflows.
* Knowledge of **real estate SaaS** or proptech products.

---

### **What We Offer**

* Competitive salary based on skills and experience.
* Flexible working hours with remote options.
* Opportunity to work with a talented, mission-driven team.
* Fast-paced environment where your work has real impact.
* Career growth and learning opportunities.
